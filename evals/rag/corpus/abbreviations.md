# Short forms used in this repository

RRF stands for reciprocal rank fusion, the method Qdrant applies when combining
dense and sparse candidate lists. It is unrelated to RFC, and unrelated to the
RRF that appears in radiology literature.

ACL means access control list throughout, never "asynchronous callback layer".
SSE is server-sent events. MRR is mean reciprocal rank, a retrieval metric, and
not the mean response rate that appears in the evaluation glossary of some
other projects.
